11 — LAYOUT & RADII
App shell. Sidebar 248px (#FBFCFC) · top bar 52px with breadcrumb · content pad 32px · chat/reading column max 780px.
Canvas. App pages pure #FFFFFF; marketing/login #F6F8F8. Never tinted app backgrounds.
Shadows. Almost none: 0 1px 2px rgba(10,25,30,0.04) on raised buttons, 0 8px 24px −12px on the composer. Depth comes from borders.
Radii. 4px badges · 6–7px chips, buttons, nav items · 8px inputs, cards-in-cards · 10–12px containers, tool cards · 16px composer, page frames · 999px pills, round buttons, human avatars.
Spacing. Base 4px; common steps 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32.
Voice. Sentence case everywhere; CAPS reserved for mono labels. No emoji outside agent avatars.
