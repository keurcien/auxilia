// OPEN-SOURCE MCP CLIENT FOR TEAMS

Ship agents to your team, not to someone's laptop.

Register MCP servers once, define agents with guardrails, and run them in the browser, in Slack, or on a schedule — all on infrastructure you control.

01 — AGENTS
Prompt, tools, subagents

Coordinators dispatch work to specialized subagents, with streaming and checkpointing.

02 — GUARDRAILS
Per-tool approval rules

Always allow, needs approval, or disabled — approvals land in chat or Slack.

03 — RUNS
Durable & scheduled

Redis-backed runs survive the browser; cron triggers run agents in the background.

04 — YOUR INFRA
Self-hosted, encrypted

AES-GCM keys, Argon2 auth, per-user OAuth tokens — all in your own Postgres.
